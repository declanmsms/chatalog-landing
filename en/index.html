01 — Product

One agent. Three jobs.

Chatalog doesn't just reply to messages — it runs the whole conversational storefront, then plugs your brand into a bigger one.

01

Sell

Product Q&A, size and stock checks, cart-building, and checkout links — handled entirely inside the chat your customer already has open. No redirect to an app or website.

  • Live catalog & stock sync
  • Cart + Xendit / payment links
  • Order status & tracking
02

Promote

Approved broadcast campaigns, back-in-stock pings, and abandoned-cart nudges that read like a message from a friend — not a blast from a brand.

  • Template-approved broadcasts
  • Abandoned cart recovery
  • Restock & drop alerts
03

Marketplace

Every brand on Chatalog sits inside a shared discovery layer. Ask another brand's agent for something you sell better, and the conversation can route straight to you.

  • Cross-brand referral routing
  • Shared storefront directory
  • Category-based discovery

Live on your number in days, not months.

Connect your number
